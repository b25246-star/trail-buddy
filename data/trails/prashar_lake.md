# Prashar Lake

SAMPLE NOTES: replace every line with what you verify yourself or hear from locals.

## Overview
Trek to Prashar Lake and its temple, roughly 40-50 km from Mandi by road. The walk starts at Baggi village and is a few hours one way. The lake sits at about 2,700 m with a floating island and a pagoda-style temple.

## Difficulty
Easy to moderate. Mostly a steady uphill path. Suitable for beginners with basic fitness. Carry water for the climb.

## Best Season
Spring (April to June) and autumn (September to November) are pleasant. Winter brings snow and cold. Avoid heavy monsoon days because roads can be blocked.

## Water and Food
Carry at least one litre per person. There are basic stalls near the road start and a rest house near the lake. Confirm locally.

## What to Carry
Sturdy shoes, a warm layer (it gets cold at the lake), rain jacket, torch, snacks, cash, a charged power bank.

## Getting There
Take a bus or taxi from Mandi towards Baggi. Ask locally for the latest timings and last return transport.

## Safety Notes
Mobile signal is patchy on the trail. Tell someone your plan. Turn back if clouds build up in the afternoon.
